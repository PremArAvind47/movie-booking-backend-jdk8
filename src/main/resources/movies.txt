Interstellar is a 2014 sci-fi film directed by Christopher Nolan. A group of astronauts travels through a wormhole near Saturn to find a new home for humanity. Time moves slower near a black hole, so one hour on a planet equals seven years on Earth.

Inception is a 2010 sci-fi thriller directed by Christopher Nolan. A thief enters people's dreams to steal secrets. His final job is to plant an idea inside a person's mind. Dreams can have many levels, and time moves slower in each deeper level.

Titanic is a 1997 romance film directed by James Cameron. A poor artist and a rich young woman fall in love on a huge ship. The ship hits an iceberg and sinks in the Atlantic Ocean.

The Godfather is a 1972 crime drama directed by Francis Ford Coppola. It tells the story of a powerful Italian-American mafia family. The youngest son slowly becomes the new head of the family.
